Changes for ECHO TRAINING:
- Extracted the two realistic whole-body meshes and their original eye meshes.
- Used multiresolution level 1 and smooth normals.
- Packed UVs into single-tile atlases and added baked skin color, normal,
  and roughness textures, plus separate eye color textures.
- Centered each body and placed its feet at ground level.
- Added a basic 19-bone FK skeleton and automatic skin weights.
- Exported self-contained, uncompressed GLB files without animation clips.

The body surfaces are the original Human Base Meshes geometry, not shapes
procedurally approximated by this application. The added rig is a basic posing
aid; it does not include individual finger, facial, or corrective muscle rigs.
Modified mesh assets and added rig data are also provided under CC0 1.0.

Skin and eye textures added by this project
=========================================
The skin albedo swatch was newly generated with the built-in image_gen tool;
it is not a photograph/scan of a real person or an asset from the Blender bundle.
Blender projects that swatch onto the body, adds subtle local color tints,
and bakes color, normal, and roughness maps. Eye colors are baked separately.
Source image, generation prompt, and baking workflow are recorded in
assets/textures/README.md and assets/textures/generation-prompt.txt.
The Blender bundle's CC0 statement above describes the original mesh assets;
it is not a statement that these new textures were supplied by Blender.
